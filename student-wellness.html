Student Wellness

Take two minutes to check in with yourself. Notice what needs attention, see what’s draining your energy, and choose one small next step. There’s nothing to score or submit.

Looking for support? Go straight to OCTC resources.

1. Notice what needs attention

The Wellness Wheel is a quick look at eight parts of life. Which area is going well? Which could use a little attention today?

  1. 1EmotionalHandling feelings and stress
  2. 2PhysicalSleep, food, movement, and rest
  3. 3SocialConnection and belonging
  4. 4FinancialMoney and basic needs
  5. 5OccupationalWork and career direction
  6. 6IntellectualLearning and curiosity
  7. 7EnvironmentalThe spaces you spend time in
  8. 8SpiritualMeaning, values, or purpose

2. Check your energy batteries

Picture five batteries. Which feels charged? Which is running low? Pick one and think about what’s draining it and what might help you recharge.

  • PhysicalWhat would help my body feel more rested or fueled?
  • MentalWhat could I write down or set aside so I can focus on one thing?
  • EmotionalWhat am I carrying, and who could I talk it through with?
  • SocialWould a little connection or a little quiet help today?
  • SpiritualWhat helps me reconnect with what matters to me?

3. Choose one small next step

Try this: “The area I want to give some attention to is ____. One thing I can do today is ____.”

That might mean taking a real break, texting someone you trust, or asking for help with one thing that feels heavy.

Make your goal doable

  • Pick one action you can control. “Take a 10-minute walk” gives you somewhere to start. “Be less stressed” doesn’t tell you what to do next.
  • Give it a time or cue. “After my last class today, I’ll walk for 10 minutes before I head home.”
  • Make a smaller backup plan. If 10 minutes won’t fit, take a two-minute stretch break. Then ask: Did it help? What would I change next time?
