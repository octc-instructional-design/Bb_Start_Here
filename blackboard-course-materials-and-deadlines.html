Start in Course Content

Open the current module in Course Content and follow your instructor’s steps in order. If you jump straight to an assignment from the calendar, activity stream, or Grades, you might miss a reading, example, or direction you need first.

How to access course content (opens in a new tab)

See what’s due

In a course: Select the Calendar tab near the top to see dates for that course. Choose Due Dates for a deadline list.

Across your courses: Select Calendar on Blackboard’s main navigation. You’ll see dates from your courses together, with course colors to help you tell them apart. Choose Due Dates for one list of upcoming deadlines.

Only items with due dates entered in Blackboard appear automatically. Check your course instructions and syllabus for other deadlines. Learn how to use the Blackboard calendar (opens in a new tab).
