Netiquette: Participate Respectfully Online

Start with KCTCS’s guide to respectful online communication.

Watch on YouTube (opens in a new tab) if the player does not load.

Read before responding, explain your ideas, and disagree with ideas respectfully. Tone can be hard to read online; pause and reread before posting. Keep personal concerns about grades or classmates in a private message to your instructor.
