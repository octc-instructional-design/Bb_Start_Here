Classroom Participation

A welcoming classroom is something we build together. You can contribute without being the most outgoing person in the room.

  • Arrive ready. Bring materials and complete preparation. If you arrive late or need to leave, enter or exit quietly.
  • Give others room to think. Let speakers finish, save side conversations for breaks, and invite different perspectives.
  • Keep devices purposeful. Silence notifications and follow class technology expectations, including approved accessibility uses. Step out quietly for an urgent call.
  • Be curious when you disagree. Ask “What led you to that conclusion?” and explain your reasoning. Question ideas respectfully.
  • Share the work and the space. Follow through on group commitments, care for equipment, and leave shared areas ready for others.
  • Talk through concerns. Ask questions when you need clarification. Discuss personal concerns privately with your instructor; seek support if something feels unsafe or disrespectful.

These are practical tips. Follow your instructor’s classroom expectations and review OCTC’s Code of Conduct and Standards of Professional Conduct (opens in a new tab) for formal guidance.
