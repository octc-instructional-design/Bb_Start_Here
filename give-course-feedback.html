Give Useful Course Feedback

Your perspective can help your instructor understand how the course is working for students. Use a course feedback forum or survey when one is available.

  • Be specific. Name the module, activity, or direction you mean.
  • Explain the effect on your learning. Describe what helped you understand or what made it difficult to proceed.
  • Suggest a practical improvement. A clearer example, a more descriptive link, or a reminder in the module may help.
  • Share what works, too. Tell your instructor which resources or approaches you hope they keep.

An example

“The example in Module 2 helped me understand the assignment. In Module 3, I was unsure whether the reflection should be a separate file. Could that step be clarified in the directions?”

Choose the right channel. Use a private message for grades, accommodations, personal circumstances, or concerns about another person. Do not include classmates’ private information in a public post.

For urgent help, contact your instructor directly. If an issue affects a deadline, reach out before it is due whenever possible. A survey or forum may not be checked in time. Feedback is an invitation to improve the course; it does not automatically change assignment requirements or deadlines.
