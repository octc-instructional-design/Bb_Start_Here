Turn in your assignment

  1. Read the directions and check what file type or submission method the assignment requires.
  2. Submit your work and confirm Blackboard shows it was received.

How to submit an assessment (opens in a new tab)

If you worked in Google Docs or Slides

Follow the assignment’s allowed file type. Upload a downloaded copy rather than pasting a sharing link, unless your instructor asks for one.

Google files to Blackboard guide

Before you start a test

Read Details & Information first. Check the instructions, number of attempts, time limit, and any required tools. If a test has one attempt, opening it just to look may start that attempt. On a timed test, the timer may keep running even if you leave and come back.
