Report Problems Early

Contact your instructor as soon as a problem affects your work—before the deadline whenever possible. Let them know while you are waiting for technical help.

What to include and what happens next

Include the course and section, assignment name, deadline, what happened, and what you tried. For a technical issue, include the exact error message or a screenshot when possible. Never share your password.

If an emergency prevents you from contacting your instructor beforehand, reach out as soon as you can. You do not need to share private details to start the conversation.

Reporting a problem does not automatically extend a deadline. Ask what to do next and follow your instructor’s directions and course policies.
