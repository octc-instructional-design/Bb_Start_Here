Use Sources and Tools Responsibly

Follow your instructor’s directions for citations, collaboration, and tools—including AI. What is allowed in one assignment may not be allowed in another.

New to MLA or APA? See Citation Help (opens in a new tab).

Give credit for words and ideas from other sources. If you are unsure whether a tool or type of help is allowed, ask before using it. Keep your notes and drafts so you can explain how you developed your work.
