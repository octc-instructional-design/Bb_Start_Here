AI Creep Meter

AI help can creep from a nudge to a rewrite in just a few prompts. Pause and ask: Who’s doing the part I’m supposed to practice?

← AI Literacy

Check what your instructor allows first. Follow the assignment’s No AI, AI-Supported, or AI-Integrated label and its specific directions. The meter shows how much of the work AI is doing; it doesn’t tell you what’s allowed. If the directions are unclear, ask your instructor before using AI.

Same assignment, four ways to ask

Imagine you’re writing a discussion post that asks you to explain your own view.

  1. 1

    Ask for questions

    “Ask me questions to help me figure out what I think.”

    Who’s doing the work? You develop the idea and write the post.

  2. 2

    Ask for feedback

    “Point out where my reasoning is unclear. I’ll revise it.”

    Who’s doing the work? You decide what to change and write the revision.

  3. 3

    Ask for a rewrite

    “Rewrite this so my argument is stronger.”

    Who’s doing the work? AI may supply reasoning or make choices you were supposed to make.

  4. 4

    Hand over the post

    “Write my post and end it with a question for my classmates.”

    Who’s doing the work? AI does the work the discussion was meant to help you practice.

Reset the chat when AI goes too far

“Stop there. Ask me one question that helps me think through this myself.”

Set aside any wording or reasoning AI supplied that the assignment asks you to develop. Go back to your own notes and decide what you want to say. For more ways to ask for a hint or feedback, see Ask for the Help You Actually Need.
