AI Tools for Students

Choose a tool based on what you want to do. You don’t need to try them all or pay for a subscription to get started.

← AI Literacy

Check your instructor’s directions first. Use AI only in ways your assignment allows. Keep private information and restricted course materials out of AI tools, and verify answers against your course materials or original sources.

Microsoft Copilot Chat

Start here with your KCTCS account. Copilot Chat is included at no extra cost with eligible Microsoft 365 Education accounts. Access and features can vary by account.

Ask for a plain-language explanation or turn assignment directions into a checklist. Compare the checklist with the original directions.

Open Copilot Chat (opens in a new tab) · Microsoft’s student access information (opens in a new tab)

ChatGPT

Try it for practice questions, hints, or step-by-step help with a concept.

In Study mode, ask for one question at a time and try answering before you ask for feedback.

Open ChatGPT (opens in a new tab) · ChatGPT Study mode guide (opens in a new tab)

Claude

Try it when you want to talk through ideas or get feedback on a draft you wrote.

Ask it to point out where a reader might get confused. Decide what to revise yourself.

Open Claude (opens in a new tab) · Claude’s overview (opens in a new tab)

Google Gemini

Try it for getting oriented to an unfamiliar topic and finding links to explore.

When Gemini shows related links, open the original pages and check whether they actually support the claim.

Open Gemini (opens in a new tab) · Gemini’s guide to related sources (opens in a new tab)
