Plan Your Week

  • Review announcements, your course schedule, and upcoming work regularly.
  • Put deadlines in one place and schedule time to read, practice, and complete assignments.
  • Start early enough to ask questions and resolve technical problems. Discussion posts and replies may have separate deadlines.
  • Check submission confirmations and return to read instructor feedback.
